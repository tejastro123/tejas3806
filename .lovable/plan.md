
# 🎨 Software Engineering Portfolio Website

A creative, playful, and professional portfolio to wow recruiters and hiring managers.

---

## 1. Hero Section
- Animated greeting with your name and title (e.g., "Software Engineer")
- A short punchy tagline
- Playful floating/animated shapes or gradient blobs in the background
- Call-to-action buttons: "View My Work" and "Get In Touch"

## 2. About Me
- A friendly bio section with your photo (you can upload one later)
- Key highlights about you — what drives you, your approach to engineering
- Fun personality touches (e.g., animated emoji, a "fun facts" mini-list)

## 3. Skills Showcase
- Visual skill cards/chips grouped by category (Languages, Frameworks, Tools, etc.)
- Playful hover effects and color-coded categories
- Progress indicators or proficiency levels

## 4. Projects Gallery
- Filterable project cards (by tech stack or category)
- Each card shows: title, description, tech tags, thumbnail image
- Links to live demo and GitHub repo
- Smooth hover animations and transitions

## 5. Experience & Education Timeline
- Visual vertical timeline with alternating left/right layout
- Company/school logos, role titles, dates, and brief descriptions
- Animated entry on scroll

## 6. Testimonials
- Carousel/slider with quotes from colleagues, mentors, or professors
- Name, role, and optional photo for each testimonial

## 7. Blog / Writing Section
- Simple card grid for articles or technical posts
- Each card shows title, date, short excerpt, and "Read More" link
- Posts will be hardcoded content (no CMS backend needed)

## 8. Contact Form (Working)
- Name, email, and message fields with validation
- Powered by a Supabase Edge Function that sends emails
- Success/error toast notifications
- Links to GitHub, LinkedIn, email, and other socials alongside the form

## 9. Design & Experience
- **Creative color palette** with vibrant accents and playful gradients
- **Smooth scroll animations** as sections enter the viewport
- **Fully responsive** — looks great on mobile, tablet, and desktop
- **Smooth navigation** — sticky header with scroll-to-section links
- **Dark/light mode toggle** for extra polish

## 10. Backend (Supabase)
- Edge Function for the contact form email delivery
- No login/auth needed — this is a public portfolio
